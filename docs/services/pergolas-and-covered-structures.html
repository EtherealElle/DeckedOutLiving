Pergolas & Covered Structures

A deck with no shade is a deck nobody sits on between June and September. Cover is what turns it back into usable space.

Pergola, pavilion, or roofed cover?

StructureWhat it doesBest for
PergolaOpen slatted top — breaks up sun, does not stop rainDappled shade, a defined outdoor “room”, something to grow a vine over
PavilionSolid roof, open sides, usually freestandingReal shade and rain cover away from the house
Attached coverSolid roof tied into the house rooflineUsing the deck in any weather; feels like part of the house

Getting the shade right

A pergola only shades when the sun is high. If the problem is late afternoon glare from the west, slats running the wrong way will not help much. Which direction the rafters run, how wide the slats are and how closely they are spaced all change how much sun actually lands on your table — and it is worth deciding that against the direction the sun actually comes from in your yard rather than picking a look from a photo.

Attached or freestanding

Attaching to the house gives you a seamless look and saves two posts, but it means tying into the existing roof and wall properly so water is directed away. Freestanding is simpler structurally and can go where the shade is actually wanted, which is not always against the building.

Matching the house

The thing that makes a structure look bolted-on is usually proportion, not colour. Post thickness, beam depth and how far the rafter tails overhang do more for whether it looks original to the house than the stain does. Heavier timbers generally read better than the minimum that would hold it up.

Common questions

Does a pergola keep rain off?

Not on its own — the top is slatted and open. If you want to sit out in the rain you want a solid roof, either a pavilion or a cover attached to the house.

Can a pergola go on an existing deck?

Often yes, but the deck framing has to be able to carry it and the posts need to land where there is support underneath. That is the first thing we check.

What about a fan or lighting?

Both are common and much easier to plan for before the structure is built than to add afterwards. Wiring runs are worth deciding early.

How much shade will I actually get?

It depends on slat width, spacing and orientation. We will talk through the trade-off between shade and the open feel most people want from a pergola.
