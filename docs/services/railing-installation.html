Railing Installation

A railing is the part of a deck people put their whole weight against without thinking. It should not move when they do.

Why railings come loose

Nearly always the same reason: the posts were fastened to the outside of the rim joist with screws rather than bolted through the framing. It feels solid the day it is built. Two seasons of wood movement and leaning later, it is not.

Doing it properly means anchoring posts to the frame itself so the load goes into the structure. It is more work at the post, and it is the difference between a railing you trust and one you warn guests about.

Options

TypeWhy people pick itWorth knowing
Pressure-treated woodCheapest, matches a pine deckNeeds sealing along with the deck; balusters can loosen over time
CompositeMatches composite decking, low upkeepHigher cost; usually uses a manufacturer’s system
AluminiumSlim, strong, very little maintenanceReads more modern than some houses want
CableKeeps the view; popular where there is one worth keepingNeeds solid, well-braced end posts and periodic re-tensioning

Fitting to an existing deck

You do not need a new deck to get a new railing. If the frame is sound, a railing swap is one of the biggest visual changes you can make to an older deck for the money — and it is a good moment to fix post anchoring that was never right to begin with.

Small details that matter

  • A graspable top rail is more comfortable than a wide flat cap on stairs.
  • Post caps keep water out of end grain, which is where wood posts rot first.
  • Stair railings need to follow the pitch cleanly — stepped railings on a sloped run look wrong and feel worse.
  • Lighting is far easier to run while the railing is apart than after it is finished.

Common questions

Can you replace a railing without touching the decking?

Usually yes. Posts may need attention where they meet the frame, but the deck surface generally stays.

How high should a deck railing be?

Height and baluster spacing are set by the requirements that apply to your deck rather than by preference. We will confirm what applies to yours when we come out.

Is cable railing a lot more expensive?

It is typically the most expensive of the four, mostly because of the hardware and the extra bracing the end posts need. It is worth it where there is a view to protect.

Can I mix materials?

Yes, and it often looks best. Aluminium balusters with a wood top rail is a common combination that keeps cost down while looking slimmer than all-wood.
